Privacy Policy

Weaver Games · Last updated 28 September 2026

In short: our games don't collect personal information. They have no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and they don't send your data anywhere. Your progress is saved on your own device. Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store, which handle the payment; we never see your payment details.

Who we are

Weaver Games makes mobile games. This policy covers every game we publish on Google Play and the Apple App Store; each one is listed below, with what it does with data. It also covers this website.

Questions or requests: weavergamesstudio@pm.me.

What our games don't do

  • They don't ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos or any other personal information.
  • They have no user accounts and no sign-in.
  • They contain no advertising and no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools, and they don't use your device's advertising identifier.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal information, because we don't have any.

What stays on your device

Each game saves your progress and settings in a file on your device. The file never leaves your device through us. Uninstalling the game deletes it. Your device's own backup, such as iCloud Backup, may include it; that backup is under your account and that service's terms, and we can't access it.

Purchases

If a game offers an in-app purchase, Google Play or the App Store processes it under their own terms and privacy policies (Google, Apple). We never receive your name, card or billing details. The game only learns which purchases your store account owns, so it can unlock them, including when you use "restore purchases" on a new device.

If you email us

If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you, and we delete it once it's no longer needed to help you. We don't add you to any mailing list.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience. Because they collect no personal information from anyone, they collect none from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access or delete personal information held about you. Our games hold none; the only information we might have is an email you sent us, and we'll delete it on request. To delete a game's saved progress, uninstall the game. Purchase records are held by Google or Apple; contact them to access or delete those.

This website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, scripts or trackers of ours. GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes to this policy

If a game's data practices change, for example if a future update adds a feature that needs data, we'll update this page before that update ships and change the date at the top. The game's section below will say what changed.
